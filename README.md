## 👾 About me

I build reliable backend systems and am now putting AI into them.

- 🏭 **Tech lead at IBM** for an investment and fixed-asset platform for a major car maker: led a team of 10, Spring Boot microservices integrated with SAP, operating costs down 20%, data-processing latency down 30%.
- 🚗 **Senior backend engineer** on an Internet of Vehicles telematics platform: Spring Cloud, Kafka, MQTT and Redis, remote vehicle control used by real drivers.
- 🎓 **MSc Computer Science** at the University of Waikato (to Oct 2027): Interpretable ML, Ethics of AI, Mobile & Wearable Computing; next up Deep Learning, ML for Data Streams and Extreme Parallel Programming.
- 🇳🇿 Based in Hamilton, open to **AI / ML engineering and senior backend roles in New Zealand**.
